# Characters: look, pose, blocking (kit-clay v2)
- Expressions: `userData.setExpr('happy'|'giggle'|'worried'|'sick'|'sleepy'|'angry'|'cry')` before `tick(t,{wave,breath})` each frame; blink automatic.
- Arms hinge at the shoulder; pose by rotation only, AFTER tick: `arm1.rotation.z` + swings the screen-right arm out/up, `arm-1.rotation.z` - the screen-left (0 down, ±2.4 overhead). `rotation.x`: hanging arms forward with negative x (-0.12 default, -1.0 holding something out), raised arms forward with positive x (~1.0); negative x on raised arms goes behind the head. Gesture with the camera-side arm.
- Held props: add to `userData.hand1` / `userData['hand-1']`; world-space follow: `g.updateMatrixWorld(true)` then `hand1.getWorldPosition(v)`. Small props read as blobs at phone size: scale up and add one coloured detail.
- `longSleeve:true` (mom, nana, wawa); `stripes:colour` paints flush. Sitting: raise position.y, no leg rig.
- Head half-widths (incl. hair): mom 1.1, dad 1.18, adult neighbour (scale 1.4) 1.18, mama 0.8, nana 0.71, wawa 0.53, kid at 0.62 scale 0.52. Same depth needs |dx| >= sum of half-widths.
- Whole-family closing (tested): parents back row (mom x -1.9, dad 1.95, z -0.7), kids front (nana -1.6, mama 0.0, wawa 1.45, z ~1.4); camera (0, 6.6, 26) look (0, 3.0, 0) fov 29. Crowds: second row on a riser (~0.95).
- Lens fov 28-30, farther camera; fov 40 close-ups bulge heads. Framing helper `FW(cx, cz, width, top, tilt)`: k = 1080/width, d = 960/k/tan(14.5°), yc = top - 130/k, pos [cx, yc + d·sin(tilt), cz + d·cos(tilt)], look [cx, yc, cz], fov 29; width = scene x-span + ~1.2, top ≈ highest head (adults 3.8-3.9, kids ~3.0) so captions sit above.
- Off-stage: |x| >= 5 from shot centre or hidden; walk-ins start outside the frame. Stages at x offsets of 60 (room 0, mechanism 60, others ±60, ±120).
- `window.__dbg = { s, camera }` at the end of update(); `node overlap.js out.html 0.5 <DUR> 0.5` — persistent overlaps/CUTs are bugs to re-block. `qa.js` does a pixel-accurate check (see its header).
- Scenes: separate stages, `VE.camAt` between them (switch key lists at the iris midpoint). Never static: bob, blink, tuft sway, breathing (fast when sick), jumps, particles, cute-faced viruses, rain, extruded hearts. Mood via `bgAt`, lights, fog; night only in the danger scene (darken room userData {floor, wall, win, wain} colours, dim key + hemi). `CK.room` props on by default (`props:false` to place your own); bunting/curtains opt-in. HUD extras (icon rows, step lists) in `hud(g,t)`; project 3D points with `Vector3.project(camera)`.
- Pitfalls: mechanism stage camera far enough for the whole tube + labels, subject inside ~half the tube diameter; stats footnote right under the caption (not on bar labels); viruses/props away from faces (z offset); the pacifier hides wawa's mouth (use brows/cheeks/eyes or `{paci:false}` for a scene); no 3D label with the caption's text; hide zero-scale and off-stage characters; no CapsuleGeometry in r128.
