# Zoom-in dioramas (history milestones) and continuous worlds

Prefer ONE persistent world the camera flies across (town / map / cosmos). Dan rejected cut-up versions twice ("เป็นท่อนๆ", "ยังไม่สมูธ"). Dioramas are for history time-lapse milestones where the map is the backbone and the HUD year keeps running.
- Each zoom is part of one trip: map camera dives to the exact spot -> engine `iris` (dur ~0.44, switch at midpoint) -> diorama -> iris back (or straight to the next). `glide` at each switch; the caption on both sides says why we go there.
- Diorama groups far from the map (x ≈ 1000 + i*60) on a floating platform (grass/stone slab + tapered soil frustum); hide the map root and other dioramas while one shows. Schedule table `[id, start, end]`; pick map vs diorama camera keys per frame. 2.5-4 s each.
- 9:16 framing: camera ~22-26 units from a 12-wide platform (local offsets ~1.9x), look target ~1.9 above the platform so the scene sits in y ≈ 800-1500 below captions. Big structures from 30+ units (a 10-wide wall fills the frame from ~25). Animate something in every diorama.
- Long flights: CatmullRom curve with one eased parameter, not a chain of keys. Camera never dead-stops (hold <= 1 s), establish high then descend, labels fade.
- Places beyond the map: 2D inset panel in the HUD (world-atlas 50m outlines, border drawing on, distance arrow) over the live map instead of a cut.
- Time stories: one forward clock in the HUD (calendar / day counter + progress bar; hours -> days -> "ผ่านไป N ปี" with log-scale bar for long aftermaths), the world changing in place.
- No objects passing through each other (heads through awnings, people inside roofs, queues through poles, two props on one spot, labels over captions). Leave clearance and look for it in every test frame.
- Map ground muted green (#46684a-ish), navy sky/fog, hemi ~0.5 + sun ~0.95 (bright light + near fog washes the map out). Terrain: gaussian peaks at real summits (~2.5x, said in a footnote), rivers carved, labels with real heights.
- Model library (build from primitives, face +x, y = ground): person with arm/leg pivots + งอบ, horse/buffalo, spoked wheels, junk, royal barge, steamer, rowboat, Thai palace (tiered roofs, spire, chofa), viharn, chedi (LatheGeometry), Lanna chedi, city wall, shophouse rows, palm/tree, carriage, horse tram, steam loco, monoplane, truss bridge with bascule, BTS/MRT, jetliner, terminal, control tower, data center (LED racks, roof fans), water wheel, overgrowth, sala with throne, crowned king, iron hell-box with glowing seams, thought bubbles as sprites. Smoke = Sprites with radial-gradient canvas. Buildings on a map as typed InstancedMeshes (stilt house, shophouse, suburban, mid-rise, glass tower with window texture), shadows on. Water = shimmer shader plane (include fog chunks); roads/rails = textured ribbons.
- Research period details before modeling (e.g. Don Mueang 2457 = Nieuport monoplanes; elephant flag before 2460; Memorial Bridge = 3 spans, lifting centre). Say up front the look is a model/toy-city style.
- Three gotchas: `group.add(mesh)` returns the group; `rotation.order='YXZ'` before tipping a figure; no CapsuleGeometry in r128; lookAt is world space (use quaternion.setFromUnitVectors in offset groups); smoothstep(a>=b) undefined; canvas overlay rects need feathering; InstancedMesh needs setColorAt before first render; draw-on lines resampled before setDrawRange; lines over dense cities `depthTest:false` + high renderOrder.
